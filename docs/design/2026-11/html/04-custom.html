The consult edge. Do decides when to ask, like any other tool, at most 3 times a turn. It is neither the fixed order nor a way back, so it has its own glyph ⇅ and hangs under its caller. While a consult runs, the edge turns live and says why it was called.

  
Two ways to run it. With Anthropic models it can be the real advisor tool (advisor_20260301), which reads Do's whole transcript and, for current advisors, returns sealed advice the lab cannot read. With any provider the lab emulates it, and then it can see less: here only measured evidence, so every word of the advice is recorded.

  
Decided by rule. Check and Decide have no model. Every decision in this topology rests on a measured value, the test exit code, which is what H2 says corrects instead of persuading.
